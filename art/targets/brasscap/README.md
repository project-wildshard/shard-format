# Brasscap Mesa golden targets

> Written by: Claude (opus-goldtargets)

> **State:** look targets, made 2026-11-11 (Cancún) for TEMPLATE-E2E-PLAN row LF-T2 in project-wildshard-meta
> `docs/plans/TEMPLATE-E2E-PLAN.md`. Mockups, not renders: nothing here is built yet.

Twenty golden target images for Template 2, Brasscap Mesa (Blender-only shooter, low-poly sunset canyon, lever rifle, tin bandits, Dust Wyrm). Each is a first-person frame in our engine's camera and
HUD, one step past the current build and still a lightweight dev-test level. The look loop
(singleplayer `docs/design/LOOK-LOOP.md`) anchors to them: build a zone, capture the same camera, measure the gap.

These are art, not product: keep them out of `corpus/` and out of any shipped pack.

## How they were made

Codex CLI `codex exec` with its built-in image_gen, one generation per file (1536x1024 desktop, 1024x1536
pillarboxed phone, cropped to its screen and resampled to 402:874), from three image inputs: a real engine shot of this level for
camera, scale and HUD, plus the approved v3 board mockups for style. Every image was looked at and
re-rolled when it read as concept art or broke the camera or HUD. Desktop files are 1400x933 JPEG;
phone files are 706x1536 JPEG (402:874).

The two Dust Wyrm fight shots (14, 15) kept drifting back to the depot, so their last roll took the
finished sand-pit frame (16) as image 1 for the setting and the v3 boss mockup for the wyrm.

Every prompt is the common block (engine camera and HUD), then the style paragraph below, then the shot.
Phone shots add the phone-frame paragraph. The exact prompts are in `prompts.json`.

## Style paragraph

STYLE "Brasscap Mesa" (Template 2, a shooter that shares NOTHING with any mossy level: no moss, no grid tiles, no size-labelled walls): a small low-poly desert canyon, flat-shaded faceted geometry like image 1 (the real Blender build), every rock a chunky faceted stepped mesa in banded terracotta, burnt orange, rust red and sand cream; saguaro cacti as simple faceted columns; a weathered wooden water tower, a rail depot shack with a tin roof and a porch, wooden fences, barrels and crates, a brass bell on a post, canvas tents with red flags. Late-afternoon SUNSET: a big sky gradient from pale gold at the horizon to turquoise at the top, a few long flat salmon-pink clouds, long violet shadows, warm dust haze. Enemies are TIN BANDITS: chunky toy robots built from tin cans, a bent stovepipe hat, a red bandana, one glowing yellow eye, a tiny name tag "TIN BANDIT" and a short thin health bar above each. The gun is a LEVER-ACTION RIFLE (walnut stock, brass receiver, clean low-poly mesh) held in first person at the lower right by a brown gloved hand, its barrel pointing at the crosshair. SHOOTER HUD extra: a small dark slate ammo pill at the TOP LEFT with a tiny weapon name over a row of small cyan segment ticks and a count. Hotbar slots: "RIFLE" and "EMPTY".
