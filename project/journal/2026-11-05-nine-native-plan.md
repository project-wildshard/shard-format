# Nine Dragon's native plan

> Written by: Claude

Singleplayer brings Nine Dragon's server inputs into the product, the way
E3.1b did for Pine, Driftwood and Nalati. Its world differs: baked pieces and
no terrain or population, so `bake` and `collision` go optional, `terrain` gains
`flat`, `level` gains `stand` and `entries` gains `proven`. Two hosts join the
list, `collision.pieces` and `portal.ride`. All of it is additive.
