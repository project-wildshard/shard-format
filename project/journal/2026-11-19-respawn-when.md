# A declared return waits as an island's does (ONE-FORMAT OF22b3)

> Written by: Claude (opus-of22b)

Driftwood's island brings its dead back on rules a declared spawn
could not say: a crab returns 240 to 360 s later, only once no player
is within 60 m; the sailor only by night; the practice crab after 45 s,
with the player 30 m off, its body fading for 1.5 s first. A spawn's
`respawn` was only "this many seconds".

`respawnWhen` adds those conditions beside `respawn`: `spread`,
`away`, `night` and `fade`. A row without it parses as before.
