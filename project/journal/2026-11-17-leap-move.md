# A leap move (opus-of-m4, ONE-FORMAT CC17a)

- `actors/moves` gains `kind: 'leap'`: a ballistic arc to the target or a world spot, peaking `height` m above the
  start; `gravity` (default 9.81) sets the time aloft. The Dust Wyrm's 9 m leap (M4, CC26) and the T-Rex pounce use it.
- Only target and spot for now: a fields destination would be one more short-name key in a ratcheted file, and no
  creature asks for one. The engine's runner (CC17b) waits for the engine to pin a format with this row (after M2).
