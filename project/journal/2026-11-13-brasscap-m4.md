# Brasscap's bandits and Dust Wyrm from the cleanroom (M4.13)

> Written by: Claude (opus-mossrender2-brass), 2026-11-13

Jake picked the cleanroom's bandits and Wyrm over engine's (meta SHARD-SOURCE-PLAN M4.13). Both GLBs are rebuilt
from `brasscap-cleanroom/src/actors.js` on the same rigs, joints, clips and glow parts, so the sim, the hit dims and
every clip test stay as they were.

- `bandit.ts`: the cleanroom's `bandit()` standing, at 0.6 scale so it stays 1.45 m (the species' hit dims are for
  that height): grey tin-can body with two dark bands on dark legs, both arms raised forward with a revolver, a red
  bandana cone, a square tin head, a wide black hat with a red band, and two amber eyes (octahedra) as the `Eye`
  part. 366 triangles, 78,816 bytes. Tin is metalness 0.45, roughness 0.45, as the cleanroom draws it.
- `wyrm.ts`: the cleanroom's `wyrm()` on BM-5's arch: sixteen plates, rust and terracotta in turn, each narrowing
  to a step at its seam, swelling to the head as the cleanroom's does, cream dorsal spikes, a flared funnel head
  with a rust lip, twelve cream teeth (the lower six ride the jaw), a near-black throat and a bigger glowing core
  (the `Gullet` part, still 8 triangles). The arch is a little shorter (segment 0.215 m) to keep 2.2 m at scale 1.
  532 triangles, 92,132 bytes. The cleanroom's belly rims cost 270 triangles over the 600 cap, so the plate steps
  stand in for them.
- The sand-ring tell's emitters take `dustCloud()`'s look: bigger (1.4 m), dimmer sand-coloured puffs that rise
  slowly and hang (they are additive in engine, so the colour is the cleanroom's dust at its opacity).
- `subjects.ts` swapped the four blobs: library +21,628 bytes compressed.

The cleanroom's worm is about four times this size in its shots. Growing it means the spawn's `scale` and the species'
dims together (a sim change), so it is left for AP23 or a later row.
