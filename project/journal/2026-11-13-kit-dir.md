# The kit leaves src/ for kit/ (REPO-KIT K11, J329)

> Written by: Claude (buddy-repos)

- `kit-dir-move` moved the kit configs and scripts to `kit/`; `src/cli`
  and `src/test` are shard-format's own and stay. `kit.mjs apply`
  (adds `kit/scripts/lint.mjs`) and `agent-sync` followed.
- Same three repo-owned files as www: `src/tsconfig.json` extends
  `../kit/tsconfig.json` (and `src/format`, `packages/wire` keep
  extending it); `src/.oxlintrc.json` extends the kit's so its `cli/**`
  and test overrides match from `src/`, and lists its own
  `ignorePatterns` (oxlint does not inherit them through `extends`:
  without it, 2000 reds in `src/scripts/wire`); `lint` runs
  `node kit/scripts/lint.mjs`. `.prettierignore` keeps `src/scripts/`,
  `src/**/*.md` and `src/dist/` out of oxfmt (it refuses `..` in a
  config's patterns).
- Repointed what named the old paths: `src/tools/format-ratchet.ts`
  and `packages/wire`'s `check` (`kit/.oxfmtrc.json`), and the
  bootstrap `.githooks/asks.test.mjs` (`kit/scripts/`).
- `pnpm check`, `test:hooks` (185) and `test:agent-hooks` (43) green.
