# Wire 1.5.0: a creature's projectile flight (opus-of-m23c)

- ONE-FORMAT CC18c: a creature's strike can launch a projectile the server sweeps for seconds, and no page saw it: wire
  1.4 had no event that carries a launch point and aim. Event 16 `flight` (`since: 5`) carries the tick, the source
  (a ref), the launch point (`at`, cell metres), the aim (`dir`, F32 per axis), `speed` and `gravity`.
- The aim is full width, not the 8-bit `died` direction: a flight is flown for seconds, and 8 bits per axis puts a 30 m
  arc a quarter metre off the server's. Flights are rare, so twelve bytes is cheap.
- A page flies the flight by the server's law (semi-implicit Euler at the sim rate), so two pages draw one flight.
- A room older than 1.5 sends none; an older page skips the record by its length and draws no flight, as before.
- The lock diff is the event plus `wireMinor` 5; the v1 byte examples are rewritten (the welcome's event table);
  `wire-v1.5.0` publishes it.
