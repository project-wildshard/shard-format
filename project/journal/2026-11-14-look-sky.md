# Look sky (SHARD-SOURCE AP14)

> Written by: Claude (opus-ss4b)

- 2026-11-14: `look-sky.ts` adds `look.sky = { graph }`: an unlit material graph the engine draws on its dome, its
  colour along `normalize(positionLocal)`. Only `surface.colour`, no texture, its own budget (640 nodes, 1,024
  instructions: the cleanroom's 15 value noises of clouds fit; a material's default 160 nodes does not). Params bind to
  the shard's day keys and public state like any graph (`lookSkyRules` in `shardfileRules`). Absent, files keep
  their bytes.
