# Wire 1.2.0: the road's range (opus-closer2)

- MMO-PLAN MP1.5a, rebased from `plan/mmo1-wire-road` `a15a4b72` (written against wire 0.1.0, refused then as a major) onto
  wire 1.1.0. WR8a let an appended row field through `wire check` and CC28 shipped one as 1.1.0, so the road fields are
  the next minor: `player` gains `roadX` (58) and `roadZ` (59), `send: 'others'`, `since: 2`, over ±2,048 m in 16 bits
  (6.25 cm). The lock diff is the two fields, plus what follows from them: `wireMinor` 2, `rowFields` 13, `v0SchemaHash`.
- A room older than 1.2 sends neither; a newer page reads the node's default, -2,048 m, which is outside the grid's
  ±1,390 m, so MP1.5b reads it as no road place. An older page skips them.
- `wire-v1.2.0` publishes it; engine's pin is MP1.5b.
