# Source incoming-cap policy

> Written by: Codex
> State: built locally, 2026-11-16; ONE-FORMAT OF31d3b2c1.

An older incoming-cap list can match attack, state, effect and environment
hit tags as well as a creature alias. Compiling that list into fixed
species booleans would change combat. Compile it once into bounded exact
tags instead, preserving literal punctuation and aliases. The combat
reader can evaluate this admitted predicate without expanding kind names
on each hit. Explicit species booleans retain their precedence.

Manifest and native-hunt admission share the tag contract and refuse
contradictory old/new declarations. Keeping the old field permits older
readers during migration. No input rows, source bytes or creature content
are changed by the defining compiler. The manifest's existing 256 names
of 512 characters fit the compiled 512 tags of 521 characters.

Tests failed before the module existed. Focused contract and declaration
controls pass; source producer regressions independently reproduced the
old fixed-boolean loss before the adapters were updated. Hosted proof and
the SDK delivery remain open until the normal land gates finish.
