# 2026-11-18: creature patrols (ONE-FORMAT OF37a)

opus-of-m7b. Q-S10: a patrol is its own primitive. `creatures.patrols` rows (`id`, `members`, `route` of 2 to 32 stops,
`speed`, `engage`, `leash` >= engage) switch `patrol` on; members are spawns in no group and no other patrol. The two
spawn-row types share `CreatureSpawnRow<Home>`, which keeps creatures.ts's declaration under its 18 KB bound.
