# A charge row and fur strands (opus-of-m9)

- OF11b: a plain strike row may carry `overshoot` and `farWeight`
  (beyond N metres it scores another weight), so Dunes' strider charge
  converts without loss and the data host can run it like the program.
- OF3c2: toon and PBR surfaces may carry `strands`, the creature
  factory's fur texture as its numbers; the kit looks get the grain the
  engine draw has, at no byte cost. Both are additive; absent keeps bytes.
