# Convex hulls in declared prop collision

> Written by: Codex (ONE-FORMAT OF21)

Nine Dragon's Banyan at the Paifang carries a 16-vertex stone hull.
The ordinary prop collision section now preserves that shape and pose.
Admission bounds local vertices and the transformed hull, requires volume
and charges each hull vertex against the existing collider budget.
Entry clearance checks the hull's posed bounds. Road and portal floor
proofs still use the declared walk surfaces.
